Today we're opening Lumen to everyone. Lumen collects product feedback from the places your customers already talk to you (support tickets, sales calls, and community threads) and groups it into themes your team can act on.

## What's in the first release

- **Inbox** pulls feedback from Zendesk, Intercom, and Slack.
- **Themes** cluster similar requests so you see the ten things people ask for, not five hundred tickets.
- **Linking** connects each theme to the roadmap item that addresses it.

## What's next

We're working on weekly digests and a public changelog. If there's something you'd like to see, reply to any Lumen email and it lands in our own inbox.
